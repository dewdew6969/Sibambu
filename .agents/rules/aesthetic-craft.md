# Rules: High-Craft Aesthetic & Anti-AI-Slop Standards

When designing, coding, or refactoring web interfaces for this project:

1. **Brand Authenticity Over Generic Templates:**
   - Anchor design in SiBambu's identity: Smart Environmental Tech, Circular Economy, Community Bank Sampah in Karawang.
   - Use intentional green and slate palettes with gold reward accents (e.g. Emerald `#15803D`, Slate `#0F172A`, Amber `#F59E0B`, Soft Cream/Paper `#F8FAFC`).
   - Avoid generic, overused purple-blue neon gradients.

2. **Rhythm & Layout:**
   - Avoid uniform 3-card rows. Use asymmetrical grids, featured spotlight cards, data tables, and organic split sections.
   - Use generous, intentional whitespace on marketing viewports and dense, clear layouts on data/admin dashboards.

3. **Typography:**
   - Pair distinct modern typography (e.g. Plus Jakarta Sans / Inter) with strict modular scale.
   - Avoid unstyled browser defaults and center-aligned large paragraphs.

4. **Micro-Interactions & UX:**
   - Give buttons and links tactile feedback on hover and press (subtle elevation, active scale down).
   - Maintain minimum touch targets of 44x44px for mobile responsiveness.
   - Ensure WCAG AA contrast ratio (> 4.5:1) for all readable text.
